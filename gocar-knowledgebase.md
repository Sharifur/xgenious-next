## 2. What GoCar Is (Positioning)

GoCar is a ready-to-launch car service marketplace platform from Bytesed. It connects end customers with mechanics and car service providers, and gives the platform owner a complete admin panel to run the business. The product is designed for entrepreneurs, garages, auto-service chains, and startups who want their own branded customer-facing app plus full operational control.

It's positioned for buyers who want to:
- Launch a car service / mechanic booking marketplace under their own brand.
- Offer customers a clean, mobile-first booking experience.
- Manage services, orders, refunds, users, roles, offers, taxes, and locations from one admin panel.
- Accept global payments (19+ gateways) and run promotions and notifications out of the box.

## 3. What Comes With GoCar

GoCar ships as two coordinated surfaces:

**Customer App.** The end-user mobile experience. Users sign up easily, select their car model, book tailored services, track orders, receive notifications, manage their profile, access support, and pay securely.

**Admin Panel.** The operator dashboard. The admin panel enables full control over services, products, orders, refunds, users, roles, offers, taxes, locations, notifications, and platform settings — designed to make platform operation seamless.

(The page also references "Explore Website" and a "Customer Demo Mobile App" link, indicating a public-facing site and a downloadable demo app for the customer side.)

## 4. Core Customer-App Features

**Select car models.** Customers easily pick their car model so the services shown are tailored to their specific vehicle.

**Book tailored services.** Customers book services customized to their car and their needs, rather than browsing a generic catalog.

**Track orders.** Real-time tracking of car service orders, with full status updates.

**Receive notifications.** Customers get instant notifications about service updates, order status, and bookings.

**19+ payment gateways.** GoCar supports 19+ payment gateways so customers can pay securely with their preferred method.

**Profile and support.** Customers can manage their profile and access in-app support.

**Real-time progress monitoring.** Customers can monitor service status and progress in real time and stay updated as work is performed.
