## 2. What ListOcean Is (Positioning)

ListOcean is a complete classified ads listing platform from Bytesed that lets owners launch a branded site for buying, selling, and browsing local listings. It bundles a public-facing web platform, an admin panel, and a user panel, and ships with built-in plugins (live chat, membership, wallet, SMS gateway) plus drag-and-drop builders for pages, menus, forms, and widgets — so operators can both run a marketplace and customize the front-end without code.

It's positioned for buyers who want to:
- Launch a classifieds/listings business under their own brand quickly.
- Monetize through memberships, featured/top listings, ads, and wallet-funded transactions.
- Customize design and content without coding (page/menu/form/widget builders).
- Operate at scale with role-based admin, reports, location/category management, and notifications.

## 3. What Comes With ListOcean

ListOcean ships as three coordinated surfaces:

**Web Platform.** The public website — designed and developed to be user-friendly and easy to use for visitors browsing or posting listings.

**Admin Panel.** A powerful operator dashboard so the admin is aware of everything happening on the platform and can take control end-to-end.

**User Panel.** An authenticated user area that gives users the tools they need to manage their account and their ads.

## 4. Essential / Headline Features

**Live Chat System.** Lets buyers contact ad owners in real time to ask about a listing. Powered by the Live Chat plugin (also available as a standalone add-on).

**Google Map.** Helps visitors find ads by location, making it easier to discover nearby listings.

**Membership Plugin.** Users can subscribe to memberships that give them benefits (such as showing their ads at the top) per plans defined by the admin.

**Wallet Plugin.** Users have a wallet balance they can spend on memberships or other transactions on the site. Both users and admins can see transaction history.

**Multiple Advertisements.** Supports image ads, script ads, and Google ads — opening monetization paths for the operator. Ad performance details (impressions, views, country, devices, OS, etc.) are visible in the admin panel for measurement.

**SMS Gateway Plugin.** Send SMS to users; users can also register or log in via mobile number.

**Drag & Drop Page Builder.** Build and customize web pages with a real-time drag-and-drop builder. No coding needed.

**Drag & Drop Menu Builder.** Design and customize navigation menus with a no-code drag-and-drop tool.

**Drag & Drop Form Builder.** Create and customize forms without coding.

**Drag & Drop Widget Builder.** Build and customize widgets for dynamic content and design enhancements.
