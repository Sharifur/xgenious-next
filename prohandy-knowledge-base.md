## 3. Core Capabilities (Deep Dive)

### 3.1 Dual marketplace model — Service Booking + Job Posting
Prohandy supports **both** monetization patterns common in on-demand service apps:

- **Service Booking (catalogue model)** — providers list services with prices and add-ons; clients add services to a cart and book.
- **Job Posting (bid model)** — clients post a custom job; nearby providers send offers; clients pick a provider.

Most clones support only one of the two. Prohandy supports both side-by-side.

### 3.2 Cart-Based Multi-Service Booking
- Clients can **book multiple services in a single transaction** via a cart.
- Each service supports add-ons, time/date selection, an order note, preferred staff selection, booking summary, and payment.
- Reduces friction for clients booking bundled services (e.g., deep clean + AC service in one checkout).

### 3.3 Map-Based Job Listings (Google Map)
- Providers see jobs **plotted on Google Maps** based on location.
- Helps providers triage jobs by proximity — essential for on-demand work.

### 3.4 Real-time Live Chat
- Live chat between clients and providers throughout the service journey.
- Provider **activity status** is visible so clients know when a provider is available to talk.

### 3.5 Push Notifications (Cross-Platform)
Push notifications fire on these events:

- Order Create
- Order Status Change
- Payment Status Change
- Support Ticket
- Offer Announcement

### 3.6 Provider Service Management
- Providers can **create service listings**, edit details, and **update availability**.
- Active management aligns supply with current demand.

### 3.7 Provider Staff Management
- Providers can manage **their own staff** under their account.
- Clients can pick a **preferred staff member** at checkout.

### 3.8 Custom Offers from Providers
- For job postings, providers send a **custom offer** with cover-letter detail.
- Clients view offer details and cover letter, accept or reject, and hire the preferred provider directly.

### 3.9 Admin Panel (Laravel)
- Full administrative control of the platform.
- **Multilingual support** out of the box.
- **Tax management.**
- **20+ payment gateways** integrated.
- Manage providers, clients, services, jobs, orders, payments, and support tickets.

### 3.10 Multilingual + Tax + 20+ Payment Gateways
- Multilingual ready for international markets.
- Tax engine built-in.
- 20+ gateways covers most regional markets out of the box.

### 3.11 REST API + Postman Collection
- The script ships with a **REST API** and a **Postman collection for documentation** (included in the Extended License).
- Useful for headless extensions, third-party integrations, or building additional clients.

### 3.12 Cross-Platform Mobile Apps (Flutter)
- Built with **Flutter**, so the same codebase produces both iOS and Android apps for the **Client App** and the **Provider App**.
- Optimized for both platforms.

## 5. The Service Booking Flow (10 Steps)

The product page documents the exact catalogue-booking sequence a client follows:

1. **Home Page** — discover featured services and categories.
2. **Service Details** — read provider profile, pricing, reviews.
3. **Add Add-Ons** — optional extras for the service.
4. **Select Time and Date** — pick the appointment window.
5. **Add Order Note** — instructions for the provider.
6. **Select Preferred Staff** — pick a specific staff member if available.
7. **View Booking Summary** — review the line items.
8. **Add to Cart or Instant Order** — bundle multiple services or buy now.
9. **Select Payment** — pick from 20+ supported gateways.
10. **Confirm Order** — provider receives a push, schedule is locked.

## 6. The Job Posting & Hiring Flow

For non-catalogue work, clients post jobs and hire providers. The flow:

1. **Job List** — client opens their existing job posts.
2. **Job Details** — client views the status of a specific job.
3. **Create Job** — client publishes a new job posting.
4. **Get Offers from Providers** — providers send custom offers.
5. **View Offer Details and Cover Letter** — client compares offers.
6. **Hire Preferred Provider** — client picks the best fit.
7. **Sit Back While Your Job Gets Done** — provider executes; chat + push keep both informed.

This is the bid-driven flow — useful when a service is bespoke and not in any provider's catalogue.

## 11. Glossary of On-Demand Service Terms

- **Service** — A pre-packaged offering from a provider (e.g., "Deep home cleaning — 3 BHK").
- **Job** — A custom request a client posts when no service in the catalogue fits.
- **Offer** — A provider's response to a job, with price + cover letter.
- **Add-On** — An optional extra attached to a service (e.g., adding a kitchen-deep-clean to a standard cleaning).
- **Provider** — The business or individual delivering the service. May have **staff**.
- **Client** — The end-user buying or hiring.
- **Cart** — A collection of services bundled for a single booking transaction.
- **Booking** — A scheduled service order.
- **Order status** — States an order moves through (created, accepted, in-progress, completed, etc.).
- **Push notification** — Real-time mobile alert.
- **Live chat** — In-app real-time messaging.
- **Map listing** — Job or service shown on Google Maps based on geolocation.
- **Postman collection** — A JSON file describing the REST API endpoints (included in the Extended License).
