## 2. What Qixer Is (Positioning)

Qixer is Bytesed's complete solution for launching an on-demand service marketplace where customers find, book, and pay for services from sellers (service providers). It ships as a web platform with admin / seller / customer panels, plus dedicated Buyer and Seller mobile apps, and includes built-in plugins (Live Chat, Job Post, Subscription, Wallet) for end-to-end operations.

It's positioned for buyers who want to:
- Launch an on-demand services marketplace (cleaning, beauty, repair, home services, events, etc.) under their own brand.
- Offer customers two ways to engage: book listed services or post jobs that providers respond to.
- Monetize via subscriptions from sellers and/or commissions on each order.
- Run the platform with role-based admin, real-time alerts, wallet, coupons, and detailed earning reports.
