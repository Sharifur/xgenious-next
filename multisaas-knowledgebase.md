## 2. What MultiSaas Is (Positioning)

MultiSaas is a multi-tenant SaaS platform from Bytesed that lets the buyer operate their own website-builder business. The operator's customers (tenants) sign up, pick a price plan, choose a theme, and get a working website in minutes. The operator monetizes by selling packages that include selected modules, themes, and payment gateways.

It's positioned for buyers who want to:
- Run a SaaS website-builder business without building the platform from scratch.
- Offer customers ready-made themes and modules (donations, events, support tickets, knowledge base, jobs, eCommerce, hotel booking, appointments, restaurants, etc.).
- Bundle and sell tiered packages with included/excluded modules and gateways.
- Support custom branding domains, multi-language, and 19+ payment gateways out of the box.
