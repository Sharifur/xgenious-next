# CRO Skill (Conversion Rate Optimization)

Increase purchases, signups, and demo clicks on any marketing or product page.

## 7 Dimensions (in order of impact)

### 1. Value Proposition Clarity
- 5-second rule: visitor must understand what it is, who it's for, why it's better — instantly
- Specificity beats vague: "Build a Fiverr clone in 48 hours" > "Build a freelancing platform"
- Use customer language from reviews, not internal jargon
- Hero subheadline must answer "so what?" for the headline

### 2. Headline Effectiveness
- Lead with outcome, not feature: "Launch your freelancing marketplace" not "Laravel script with 40+ features"
- Include social proof in headline when possible: "10,000+ installs"
- Specificity pattern: [number] + [outcome] + [timeframe/context]

### 3. CTA Placement, Copy, Hierarchy
- Primary CTA above the fold always
- CTA copy: value-focused ("Get Xilancer — $59") not generic ("Buy Now")
- Sticky CTA bar for long pages
- Secondary CTAs: "View Demo", "See Pricing" — reduce friction before purchase
- One primary action per section, no competing CTAs

### 4. Visual Hierarchy & Scannability
- F-pattern reading: most important content top-left
- Headers scannable in isolation — must tell the story without body text
- Screenshots/demo images close to relevant copy
- Feature icons/checkmarks for rapid scanning

### 5. Trust Signals & Social Proof
- Star rating + review count near top (not just bottom)
- Named reviews with real usernames (CodeCanyon handles = authentic)
- Specific outcomes in testimonials: "launched in 2 days", "support fixed in 1 hour"
- Logos or badges: CodeCanyon Elite, PHP, Laravel, etc.
- "10,000+ installs" stat near purchase CTA

### 6. Objection Handling
- Price objection: show savings (original vs. discounted), payment context ("one-time, no monthly fees")
- Trust objection: money-back, support period, update policy
- Fit objection: "Works on any Linux VPS", "No coding required to configure"
- Complexity objection: "Full documentation + video guides included"
- FAQ section must address top purchase blockers

### 7. Friction Points
- Too many steps to understand pricing → add pricing table early
- No demo link in hero → add "Explore Demos" CTA
- Unclear what's included → feature checklist with ✓/✗
- Mobile: CTA thumb-reachable, text not too small

## Page-Specific: Product/Sales Page
- Hero: product name + outcome headline + 1 screenshot + 2 CTAs (Buy + Demo)
- Social proof band immediately below hero
- Features: benefits-first, not feature names
- Pricing section: clear tiers, recommended tier highlighted, FAQ below pricing
- Final CTA: repeat primary purchase CTA with urgency element

## Quick Wins (implement first)
1. Add "10,000+ installs" near purchase CTA
2. Star rating visible above the fold
3. "One-time purchase — no monthly fees" next to price
4. Demo link in hero
5. Money-back or support guarantee badge
