## 2. What SafeCart Is (Positioning)

SafeCart is a Laravel-based multi-vendor eCommerce marketplace from Bytesed. It bundles a powerful admin panel, a vendor panel, a customer panel, three Flutter mobile apps (Customer, Vendor, Delivery Man), and a set of operational plugins (Live Chat, Refund, POS, Delivery Man) so an operator can run a full-featured marketplace including online + in-person sales and last-mile delivery.

It's positioned for buyers who want to:
- Launch a multi-vendor eCommerce marketplace (or a single-vendor shop) under their own brand.
- Ship native-feeling mobile experiences (Flutter source code for Customer, Vendor, and Delivery Man apps).
- Operate end-to-end: catalog, inventory, orders, refunds, POS, delivery, payments, wallets, and campaigns.
- Sell globally across 26+ payment gateways.

### Single-vendor or multi-vendor?
The page explicitly states SafeCart supports both: "SafeCart caters to both single-vendor businesses and dynamic multi-vendor marketplaces." The admin can run their own shop, vendors can register, and vendors get their own panel to manage their business and products.
