## 3. Core Capabilities

### 3.1 Drag-and-Drop Page Builder (no code)
- Build pages by dragging widgets onto the canvas.
- **180+ pre-made page-builder widgets** to compose layouts.
- Full control over the structure of the site and individual pages.

### 3.2 Drag-and-Drop Menu Builder
- Drag any menu item to reorder it.
- Convert items to dropdowns or **mega menus**, with selectable mega-menu items.

### 3.3 Drag-and-Drop Widget Builder (footer / widget areas)
- Includes **9 pre-made widgets** for footer / widget areas.
- Supports **raw HTML widgets**, newsletter widget, image widget, and more.
- Full control over widgetized regions.

### 3.4 Drag-and-Drop Form Builder (no code)
- Field types: text, number, email, select, checkbox, textarea, file upload, etc.
- Per-field "required" toggle.
- Customize any form on the site without coding.

### 3.5 Multiple Home Page Variants
- Multiple ready-made home page demos (some marked **New**, others **Coming Soon**).
- Pick a variant that matches your industry and customize from there.

### 3.6 17+ Payment Gateways
Built-in support includes:

- PayPal
- Stripe
- Paytm
- Razorpay
- Mollie
- Flutterwave
- Paystack
- Cashfree
- Instamojo
- Midtrans
- Mercado Pago
- Bank Payment (manual)
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Check Payment
- *…and additional gateways*

This breadth makes Nexelit suitable for global commerce — including India (Paytm/Razorpay/Instamojo/Cashfree), Africa (Flutterwave/Paystack), Latin America (Mercado Pago), Southeast Asia (Midtrans), and the Western markets (PayPal/Stripe/Mollie).

### 3.7 Translate Ready (Multilingual) + RTL
- Fully translate-ready — admins can add any language they want.
- **RTL (right-to-left) supported**, configurable per language.
- Admin chooses the default language and direction during language creation.

### 3.8 Newsletter Module
- Subscribers double-opt-in via an email verification flow.
- From the admin panel, send email to a single subscriber or to the entire list.
- Useful for ongoing marketing and content updates.

### 3.9 Color & Branding Settings
- Change every color of the website from the admin panel — no CSS edits required.

### 3.10 Advanced SEO Optimization
- Set meta title, meta description, and meta image globally.
- Per-product / per-page SEO fields so each item shares well on social platforms.
- **Sitemap generation** built into General Settings → Sitemap to boost crawlability and SEO score.

### 3.11 User Accounts & Profiles
- Users can change their password and personal info.
- Saved shipping addresses (no need to re-enter at checkout).
- Order history, **reorder**, and ability to pay pending payments from their dashboard.

### 3.12 Social Login
- One-click login with **Facebook** or **Google**.
- Option at checkout to create an account from order details.

### 3.13 Advanced Tax System
- Create unlimited tax types.
- Vary tax by **country and state**.
- Tax can be a **percentage** or a **fixed amount**.

### 3.14 Email Notifications
- Admin receives an email with full order details on every new order.
- Customer receives an email with order and payment information after checkout.

### 3.15 Compatibility
- Responsive on **desktop, laptop, and mobile**.
- Compatible with all major browsers.
- Comes well documented.
