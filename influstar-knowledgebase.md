## 2. What Influstar Is (Positioning)

Influstar is a ready-to-launch script — not a hosted SaaS — that gives entrepreneurs, agencies, and startup founders the full source code to operate their own branded influencer hiring marketplace. Brands can post campaigns, influencers can submit proposals, and the platform handles secure payments, communication, reviews, and payouts end-to-end.

It is positioned for buyers who want to:
- Launch a niche or general influencer marketplace quickly (in days, not months).
- Own their platform, branding, and customer relationships.
- Monetize via commissions, subscriptions, and listing fees.
- Avoid building an influencer marketplace from scratch.

## 3. What Comes With Influstar

Influstar ships as three coordinated surfaces, all included.

**Web Platform.** A complete online marketplace where influencers and brands connect, collaborate, and manage campaigns. Profile creation, browsing, proposals, and project delivery are all handled in one place.

**Admin Panel.** A real-time admin dashboard that lets the platform owner manage users, campaigns, transactions, and platform settings, and monitor performance at a glance.

## 4. Core Marketplace Features (the "everything you need to launch & run" set)

**Submit Proposal.** Influencers pitch services directly through the platform, set their rates, define delivery timelines, and attach examples so brands can compare and choose the right creator.

**Available Campaigns.** Brands post campaign opportunities; influencers browse them by niche, budget, and audience. This keeps the marketplace active and engagement high.

**Order List.** A dashboard view for both sides showing orders, deadlines, and deliverables — designed to build trust and ensure smooth project delivery.

**Custom Offer.** Brands can send tailored, one-off offers directly to influencers, creating more deal flow and additional commission revenue for the platform owner.

---

## 6. Trust & Safety Features

**Built-in Escrow System.** Milestone-based escrow protects both sides. The escrow flow is:

1. Payment funded by client.
2. Funds pending in influencer wallet until work is delivered.
3. Influencer submits completed work.
4. Client reviews and approves the work.
5. Payment released to influencer wallet.

**Withdraw Money.** Multiple payout options are available so influencers can access their earnings quickly and securely.

**Client Review.** After-project reviews cover skills, communication, and reliability, helping the platform maintain credibility and helping brands choose creators.
