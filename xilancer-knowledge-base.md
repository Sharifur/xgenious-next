## 3. Core Capabilities (Deep Dive)

### 3.1 Dual marketplace model — fixed-rate jobs *and* hourly-rate jobs *and* gigs
- **Fixed-Rate Jobs** — clients post a job with a defined budget; freelancers submit proposals.
- **Hourly-Rate Jobs** *(marked New)* — clients post hourly contracts; freelancers bill against agreed-upon hours.
- **Project / Gig Catalogue** — Fiverr-style gig listings created by freelancers; clients buy from the catalogue directly.
- **Custom Orders** — bespoke quotes and deliverables outside a standard gig.

This combination is unusual: most clones do **either** Fiverr **or** Upwork. Xilancer covers both models in one platform.

### 3.2 Advanced Account Creation (Freelancers)
- Freelancers can build a rich profile: skills, experience, portfolio, rates, availability, and more.
- Designed to "showcase multi-information" so clients quickly assess fit.

### 3.3 Project / Gig Listing System
- Freelancers can publish reusable gigs — title, description, packages, delivery time, gallery — exactly the Fiverr model.
- Clients can also browse a catalogue and **filter and order** directly.

### 3.4 Fixed-Rate Job Feeds with Smart Filters
- A job feed for browsing fixed-rate jobs.
- **Advanced Job Filters** — Category, Job type, Experience level, Budget range, Minimum work hours, Project lengths, Job by countries, Number of proposals, and more.

### 3.5 Hourly-Rate Jobs (New)
- A new addition expanding the script from gig/fixed-rate-only into an Upwork-style hourly model.

### 3.6 Live Chat System (Real-time)
- Clients and freelancers collaborate in-platform via live chat.
- Keeps communication on the platform — protecting platform-fee revenue and improving dispute resolution.

### 3.7 Proposal Submission System
- Freelancers submit a proposal on a client's job containing:
  - **Proposal amount**
  - **Delivery duration**
  - **Cover letter**
- Clients compare proposals and choose the best fit.

### 3.8 Built-in Wallet
- Both freelancers and clients have a **balance wallet**.
- **Wallet deposit** — clients top up.
- **Withdraw wallet balance** — freelancers cash out.
- **Refund money** — supports refund flows in dispute / cancellation cases.
- Freelancers can also use wallet balance to **buy membership subscriptions**.

### 3.9 Milestone Payments
- Clients can pay freelancers in **milestones** upon mutual agreement.
- **Milestone-based work** structure.
- **Milestone money deposit** so funds are held pending delivery.
- **Automatic milestone approval** option.

This is the Upwork-style escrow/milestone flow that gives clients confidence and gives freelancers payout assurance.

### 3.10 Two-Way Feedback & Ratings
- Freelancers and clients can leave **feedback on each other**.
- **Detailed feedback on multiple segments** — not a single global star, but multi-criteria reviews.
- This is essential for marketplace trust.

### 3.11 Subscription & Trial Period (Paid Memberships for Freelancers)
- Freelancers can buy a **membership subscription** to unlock premium features and more **connects** for submitting proposals.
- A **trial period** is supported.
- This is a recurring-revenue lever for the platform owner — the same model Upwork uses with "Connects."

### 3.12 Qualification Matching
- The platform **matches freelancer qualifications to client job requirements** to make candidate selection easier — reducing low-fit applications.

### 3.13 Clients' Hiring Rate Visibility
- When sending proposals, freelancers can see a client's **hiring rate** — how often this client actually hires after posting jobs.
- Helps freelancers prioritize high-value opportunities.

### 3.14 Clients' Job Activities Visibility
- Freelancers can see what's happening in a job (e.g., # of proposals already submitted, recent activity) **before** they apply.
- Reduces wasted proposal effort.

### 3.15 Custom Orders
- Bespoke deliverables outside standard gig packages — useful for negotiated work that doesn't fit a fixed catalogue.

### 3.16 Mobile Apps (iOS + Android)
- **Freelancer Mobile App** and **Client Mobile App** are part of the Complete Package tier (and are built/published as part of the Complete Launch Bundle).
- The apps mirror core marketplace flows on phone.

## 9. Workflow Walkthroughs

### 9.1 Freelancer workflow
1. Register and complete the **advanced freelancer profile** (skills, portfolio, rates).
2. Optionally buy a **membership subscription** for more proposal connects and premium features.
3. Either:
   - **Create gigs** (Project/Gig Catalogue) for inbound buyers, **or**
   - **Browse the job feed** with smart filters and **submit proposals** (amount, delivery duration, cover letter) on fixed/hourly jobs.
4. Once hired, collaborate via **live chat**, deliver against **milestones**, and request payments through the **wallet**.
5. Get paid into the wallet → **withdraw**.
6. Receive **multi-segment ratings** and feedback that build long-term credibility.

### 9.2 Client workflow
1. Sign up and **deposit funds** into the wallet.
2. Either:
   - **Browse the gig catalogue and order directly**, **or**
   - **Post a fixed-rate or hourly-rate job** and review incoming proposals.
3. **Compare proposals** with delivery duration, amount, and cover letter; check the freelancer's profile, ratings, and qualifications.
4. Hire and structure work as **milestones** with deposits.
5. Approve milestones (manually or with auto-approval).
6. Leave **detailed feedback** across multiple segments.
7. Refund handling supported when something goes wrong.

### 9.3 Admin workflow
1. Configure **commission / platform fee** model.
2. Create **subscription plans** (with trial) for freelancers.
3. Moderate listings, jobs, gigs, and disputes.
4. Configure **promotional ads** and **freelancer levels**.
5. Manage payouts, refunds, and wallet operations.
6. Use the support / community surfaces to engage users.

## 10. Glossary of Marketplace Terms

- **Gig** — A pre-packaged service offering created by a freelancer (Fiverr-style).
- **Job post** — A request for work created by a client (Upwork-style).
- **Fixed-rate job** — A job with a single defined budget for the deliverable.
- **Hourly-rate job** — A job billed by tracked hours.
- **Proposal / Bid** — The freelancer's offer on a client's job, with amount + delivery duration + cover letter.
- **Connect** — A unit consumed when sending proposals; replenished via paid membership.
- **Milestone** — A delivery checkpoint tied to a partial payment.
- **Escrow** — Funds held by the platform until milestones are delivered.
- **Wallet** — Built-in account balance for deposits, payouts, and refunds.
- **Custom order** — A negotiated deliverable outside a standard gig.
- **Hiring rate** — The percentage of a client's posted jobs that result in actual hires.
- **Job activities** — Public signals of what's happening on a job (e.g., # of proposals).
- **Freelancer level** — Tiered status (e.g., Level 1 / Level 2 / Top Rated) earned through performance.
- **Paid membership** — Subscription that unlocks premium features and more connects.
